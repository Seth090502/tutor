# English craft (original notes, V1.0 seed)

Core idea: a sentence makes a claim; a paragraph proves it; an essay chains proofs.

- Sentence core: subject + verb must agree (Neither ... is, They ... are).
- Punctuation: comma after intro phrase; apostrophe for possessive (dog's); colon after complete clause before list.
- Thesis: claim + because + roadmap. Weak: "This essay is about dogs." Strong: "Cities should fund shelters because they cut strays, disease, and costs."
- Evidence: claim -> quote/paraphrase -> analysis (how it proves + why it matters). Never quote-drop.
- Rhetoric: ethos (credibility), pathos (emotion), logos (logic). Fallacies: bandwagon, strawman (distort then attack).

Revision check: every paragraph starts with a specific topic sentence; every quote has 2+ sentences of analysis.
